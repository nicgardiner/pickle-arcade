Field Manual

MOVE — WASD or Arrow Keys  ·  AIM — Mouse  ·  FIRE — Left Click or Space
PAUSE — Esc or P (campaign only)

Shells ricochet off hard walls but expire after traveling a set distance — count your bounces.
One hit destroys any tank. That includes your own shell coming back around.
Cracked walls (crates, sandstone, brick) absorb shells and crumble after 1–3 hits.
Pits stop tanks, but shells sail right over them.
Shells that collide head-on destroy each other — use it to parry.

Multiplayer — 2–4 players online. Last tank rolling takes the round; first to 5 rounds wins the match. After the final kill there's a 2-second cooldown — die to a stray shell in that window and the round is a draw.
