    HOW TO PLAY

    THE FLIGHT

    You ride the inside wall of the tube. Steer around it to thread the gaps in the grid — one touch ends the run. Every metre flown is a point, and the tube gets faster with every zone you clear.

    SCORING

    DISTANCE1 point per metre. ZONEA new zone every 1,000 m — new colours, more speed.

    CONTROLS

    ← → / A DHold to steer. On touch, hold the left or right half of the screen. Enter / SpaceLaunch a run. Esc / PPause. MMute audio. CSteady cam — the world rolls, the camera stays upright.
