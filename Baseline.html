How to Play

You stand at the baseline, viewed from behind. Your hitbox is the glowing bar in front of you — it works just like a Pong paddle.
  • MOUSE slides you along the baseline.
  • As the ball nears you swing automatically. Bar covers the ball → you connect; miss it → you whiff the point.
  • Where it meets your bar sets the angle — centre goes straight, the edges go cross-court.
  • Every exchange is faster than the last. Long rallies get the crowd on its feet.
  • Serving: aim with the mouse, then CLICK or SPACE.

Scoring

Points → Games → Match.

Win a rally — get the ball past your opponent — to score a point. Points climb tennis-style 0 · 15 · 30 · 40. Win a game by reaching 4 points with a two-point lead — at 40–40 it’s deuce: take the advantage point, then one more.

First to 4 games wins the match (no win-by-two for the match). Serve alternates each game. Tournament finals are best of 3 sets — win 2 sets, each first to 4 games.
