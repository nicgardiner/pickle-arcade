ScribbleSled 🛷

Draw a track, press play, and watch the little sledder ride your scribbles. Gravity does the rest.

Tips

Track lines are solid walls from both sides — the sledder can ride the top, bounce off the bottom, and never pass through. Hit something too hard… wipeout. Free draw keeps as many named sketches as you like — the one you're on auto-saves as you draw, and the folder button opens the rest so you can switch, rename, duplicate, export or delete them. Built levels and campaign stars save on this machine too.
