HOW TO PLAY

Six steps and you are on the wave

01Teams take turns. Each turn one player is the Psychic 🔮 — the role passes down your team's roster.
02The Psychic draws a spectrum card — say Cold food ◀▶ Hot food — and secretly sees the target hidden on the wheel.
03They send one clue that sits exactly where the target is. Soup? Lava cake? You decide.
04Their team drags the dial to where they think the target hides, then locks it in.
05The other team steals: they call whether the true target is LEFT or RIGHT of the dial for a bonus point.
06The wheel opens. Closer = more points. First team to the target score wins the night.

ON-AIR RULES
  • One idea. A word or a short phrase — not a list, not a sentence with commas.
  • No numbers. No digits and no number words — “7”, “seven”, “a dozen” are all out.
  • No left / right. Don't steer the dial, and don't just read the card back.
  • Meaning only. No rhymes, spelling, letter counts or gestures.
  • Aim it. Your clue should land on the target, not merely near that end.
