How to jam

Eleven spots, one groove. Nothing you do can sound wrong.

  • Tap a performer — they start playing. Tap again to stop.
  • ‹ arrows › on each nameplate — change that performer's own style.
  • Tap the badge at the left of a nameplate — swap that spot for any kind of performer. Three drummers? Two singers with different voices? Go on.
  • + Add — four empty spots stand behind the band. Tap one to bring somebody new on, or pick Nobody to send them home again.
  • Drag a performer onto another to swap their places — or onto an empty spot to move them there.
  • BUILD — energy ramps up, then drops into a chorus.
  • BREAK — everything strips back, then slams in again.
  • FINISH — four bars, one big hit, and the song ends.
  • Shuffle rolls a random band. Clear stops everyone.
  • 💾 Bands — name and keep the line-up you have right now, then bring it back any time. Saving under a name you already used overwrites that one.
  • ⚙ Settings — volume, mute and a chunky pixel filter.
  • Esc (or the pause button) — pause the band, change settings or exit.
