🎬 Picking a scene

Look for a stretch where people are talking — a row, an interview, a bad line reading. 20–60 seconds is the sweet spot: long enough to be a bit, short enough to finish in one go. You can go up to 90, but everyone dubs every second of it.

Click or drag the timeline to scrub · I starts the scene at the playhead · O ends it there · or drag the teal handles · type an exact timestamp in either box.

✂️ Where breaks go

Put a break wherever the speaker changes, and after a line or a group of lines that belong together. You dub one clip per take, so each clip should be one mouthful. 4–10 seconds a clip works best.

Click the timeline to move the playhead · S (or ✂️) splits there · double-click the timeline to split on the spot · drag a marker to move a break · click a break for ◀ ✕ ▶ — nudge it 0.05s either way, or remove it.

🚫 Off mic

Some of a scene isn't yours to say. Take a clip off mic and the recording session skips straight past it — but it's still in the film, playing with the original audio.

🤐Dead air. A long pause, a walk across the room, the bit before anyone speaks. Cut it out of the session, keep it in the cut.
🎭Play one character. Leave the other actor's lines off mic and they keep their own voice — so you're acting opposite them, not doing both.
👥Split the room. Off-mic clips are the gaps a second person can dub later.
Put the playhead in a clip and press M (or the 🚫 button) · every clip row has its own 🎙️ / 🚫 toggle · off-mic clips go grey and striped on the timeline.
