How to Play

W A S D  Move

🖱️  Mouse  Look around

E  Interact — extinguish candles, open doors, enter/exit hiding spots

M  Toggle map    P / Esc  Pause

Candles keep the entity at bay — three on the first floor, four on the second, five on the last. Extinguish them all to unlock the exit door, then descend.

The entity patrols the manor. It will investigate extinguished candles and chase you on sight. Candle light repels it — stay near the flame.

Hide inside cabinets to break line of sight. If it sees you enter, it will not be fooled.

Each floor grows larger and darker — and the entity grows bolder the deeper you go. Beyond the third floor's door lies the way out.
