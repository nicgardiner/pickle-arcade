Operator manual

How to play

  • Pick a module from the tray (or keys 1–9) and hover the station to preview its points.
  • Neighbours matter. Green chips help, red chips hurt. The highlighted squares show exactly what's in range. Points lock in the moment you place, so order matters.
  • Links: modules that like each other and sit side by side grow walkways between them (+1 each).
  • Starlight: the sun floats beyond the station's edge. Tall modules cast shadows away from it — shaded tiles are darkened on the deck.
  • Two sides: ▲ Topside modules sit on the deck, ▼ Underside modules hang beneath it. Press Tab to flip the whole station over. What's below affects what's above.
  • Cross-side markers: modules that affect the other side leave a ring with their icon on the opposite face. With a module selected the rings turn green (helps it), red (hurts it) or blue (neutral) — see the key in the corner.
  • Walkway bridges join separate platforms — nothing can be built on them, and they count as open space for edge modules.
  • Merges: three of the same Hab Tower, Hydroponics, Solar Array, Reactor, Refinery or Cargo Hold touching in a line or L fuse into a landmark worth bonus points.
  • Districts: linked clusters glow in their own neon colour.
  • Supplies: hit score milestones to choose a new supply pack. When modules or space run out, the station is done.
  • Objectives: every campaign station has two or three objectives (top right) that pay bonus points. Once you earn your first ★ you can Launch station to finish early.
  • Endless Drift: reach each rising supply target before your modules run out, or the run ends. Some drops offer expansion pods — rotate with R / right-click and attach them touching the station or 1–2 squares away. Contracts pay bonus points.
  • Camera: hold the mouse wheel, right-drag or Shift+drag to turn and tilt · Q/E turn 45° · left-drag to pan · wheel to zoom · Home frames the whole station · P photo mode.
  • Z undo the last placement (not after a merge, objective or supply drop) · Esc deselect / menu. The game autosaves after every move.
