How Doodle Tunes works

  • ✏️Draw anything on the page. The playhead sweeps across and every line it touches sings.
  • ↕Higher on the page = higher note. Notes snap to a friendly scale, so nothing clashes.
  • →Left to right is time. The page loops, so keep adding to it while it plays.
  • 🎨Pen colour is the instrument; a bigger pen plays louder.
  • 〰A flat line holds a note (or pulses, for plucky pens). A slope plays a run. A dot is one note. A straight up-and-down line strums a chord.
  • 🥁Drums, bottom to top: kick, snare, clap, hi-hat.
