心得

THE WAY OF THE CLEAN CUT

一 · Draw

A shape hangs before you. Click and drag to draw your cutting line — it extends clear across the shape. On multi-cut shapes, Z / right-click undoes a line until the final cut falls. The final cut confirms them all — steel does not un-swing.

二 · Divide

Split each shape into equal pieces. Early trials ask for a clean half; later ones demand three or four pieces from as many cuts. Equal mass is everything.

三 · Ascend

Five shapes per trial, graded F to A on your total. A C unlocks the next trial. Duels are nine rounds, first to five, 15 seconds a cut — online against a rival, or local on one screen: player one cuts, hands over, player two cuts, then both blades fall together.
